About

Built in Abu Dhabi, for markets that move on macro

Analytica Horizon Technology builds analytics technology for macroeconomics and commodity futures. We're registered in Abu Dhabi Global Market, between the energy producers of the Gulf and the financial centers of Europe and Asia.

Why we exist

Macro data is public. Making sense of it quickly isn't.

Central banks, statistics agencies, energy regulators and futures exchanges publish thousands of data points every week. Each release arrives on its own schedule, in its own format, often revised later. Most teams either spend analyst hours stitching it together or rely on a handful of headline numbers.

We built the plumbing once, added models that score what matters, and put analysts on top to check the conclusions. The result is a continuous view of where the economy and commodity markets stand, and what has changed since yesterday.

Principles

How we work

Source everything

Every figure is traceable to the agency or exchange that published it, with its release date.

Explain the scoring

Rules and models are documented, so clients can challenge a conclusion instead of trusting it blindly.

Keep humans in the loop

Models flag changes; analysts decide what they mean before anything reaches a research note.
