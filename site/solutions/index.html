Solutions

Analytics for teams that trade the macro cycle

Funds, trading desks, commodity producers and corporate treasury teams use our tools to track economic conditions and futures positioning in one place, without building the data plumbing themselves.

01 · Platform

AI analytics platform

A single workspace for macro conditions and commodity futures, with models that score every indicator and flag when the picture changes.

  • Live Macro Monitor with conditions, triggers and flows scoring
  • Commodity positioning analytics across energy, metals and agriculture
  • Regime tracking: see which indicators changed status and when
  • Alerts when a reading crosses a threshold you care about
  • Full history and source for every data point

02 · Research

Research subscriptions

Plain-language analysis of what the data is showing, written for decision makers who don't have time to read every release.

  • Weekly macro and commodities outlook
  • Notes on major releases: CPI, payrolls, FOMC, CFTC positioning, FINRA margin data
  • Monthly deep dives on energy, metals and agricultural markets
  • Charts and data behind every note

Research is general market commentary. It does not take account of any reader's objectives or circumstances and is not investment advice.

03 · Data

Data and API

The same cleaned series and model scores that power our dashboards, delivered as JSON for your models, spreadsheets and internal tools.

  • Indicator values, statuses and history in one consistent format
  • Release dates and source attribution on every series
  • Status-change events for alerting and backtests
  • Stable schema with versioned changes

A public sample of the feed is available at /data/indicators.json.

04 · Engagements

Institutional analytics engagements

For teams that need something specific: we design and build models, dashboards and data pipelines around your markets and your process.

  • Custom indicator sets and scoring models
  • Commodity exposure dashboards for producers and consumers
  • Data engineering: ingesting, cleaning and versioning public and licensed data
  • Model review and documentation
