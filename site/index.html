What changed

    Margin debt history

    Customer debit balances in securities margin accounts at FINRA member firms. Borrowing tends to peak near market tops (2000, 2007, 2021) and falls fast in selloffs.

    How this board works

    Conditions, triggers and flows

    Conditions (valuation, leverage) show how far the market could fall. Triggers (rates, credit, earnings, breadth) usually decide when a decline starts. Flows show who is buying and selling.
