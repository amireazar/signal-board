    Speculative positioning

    CFTC Disaggregated Commitments of Traders, futures only

    Net long minus short positions held by managed money (hedge funds and commodity trading advisors), as a share of open interest, ranked against the past three years. Crowded positions tend to unwind sharply when the story changes.

    Why ratios, not prices

    A price mixes many forces at once. A ratio cancels out what two markets share, such as the dollar or general inflation, and isolates what differs between them: growth versus safety, supply versus demand.

    How often it updates

    Energy, rates, dollar and equity relationships update every business day. Metals and grains use the World Bank's monthly price series. Positioning is weekly, from the CFTC.
