Commodity futures, prices and positioning

Energy prices show what the physical market is paying. Positioning shows how far speculative money has already leaned one way. When positioning is stretched, prices tend to react more sharply to surprises.

Managed-money positioning

CFTC Disaggregated Commitments of Traders, futures only

Net long minus short positions held by managed money (hedge funds and commodity trading advisors), as a share of open interest. The percentile compares the latest week with the past three years: high readings mean crowded longs, low readings mean crowded shorts.

Why positioning matters

Crowded trades unwind fast. A market where speculators are already heavily long needs ever-better news to keep rising, and a small disappointment can trigger forced selling.

How often it updates

Daily spot prices post each evening, and weekly retail fuel prices on Mondays. The CFTC publishes positioning on Friday afternoons, covering positions as of the Tuesday before.
