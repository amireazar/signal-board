What changed

    Margin debt history

    Customer debit balances in securities margin accounts at FINRA member firms. Borrowing tends to peak near market tops, as in 2000, 2007 and 2021, and falls quickly in selloffs.

    Conditions, triggers and flows

    Conditions (valuation, leverage) show how far markets could fall. Triggers (rates, credit, earnings, breadth) tend to decide when a decline starts. Flows show who is buying and selling.
