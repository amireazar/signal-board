Why we exist

Macro data is public. Making sense of it quickly isn't.

Central banks, statistics agencies, energy regulators and futures exchanges publish thousands of data points every week. Each release arrives on its own schedule, in its own format, often revised later. Most teams either spend analyst hours stitching it together or rely on a handful of headline numbers.

We built the plumbing once, added models that score what matters, and put analysts on top to check the conclusions. The result is a continuous view of where the economy and commodity markets stand, and what has changed since yesterday.

How we work

Source everything

Keep humans in the loop
